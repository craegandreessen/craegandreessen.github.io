# Paloma & Sage Brand Design Guide
## Rugged Refined Aesthetic

---

## Executive Summary

This brand design guide transforms Paloma & Sage from a soft, handwritten aesthetic into a **rugged, polished masculine** brand that honors craftsmanship, heritage, and authenticity. The design balances raw, earthy elements with refined sophistication—think hand-forged tools, premium leather goods, and artisan distilleries.

---

## 1. Color Palette Strategy

### Primary Colors

#### **Charcoal Slate** - `#2C3539`
- **Usage**: Primary text, headers, navigation
- **Psychology**: Grounded, strong, sophisticated
- **Inspiration**: Forged steel, volcanic rock

#### **Burnt Sienna** - `#A0522D`
- **Usage**: Primary accent, CTAs, hover states
- **Psychology**: Warmth, earth, craftsmanship
- **Inspiration**: Aged leather, clay pottery, desert landscapes

#### **Iron Rust** - `#8B4513`
- **Usage**: Secondary accent, borders, dividers
- **Psychology**: Heritage, authenticity, raw materials
- **Inspiration**: Oxidized metal, saddle brown leather

### Secondary Colors

#### **Stone Sage** - `#8F9779`
- **Usage**: Subtle backgrounds, secondary text
- **Psychology**: Natural, balanced, organic
- **Inspiration**: Weathered sage, lichen on stone, desert flora

#### **Sandstone Beige** - `#D4C4A8`
- **Usage**: Light backgrounds, cards, overlays
- **Psychology**: Warm neutral, approachable
- **Inspiration**: Canyon walls, natural linen, raw cotton

#### **Deep Forest** - `#3D5A3C`
- **Usage**: Success states, nature elements
- **Psychology**: Growth, stability, tradition
- **Inspiration**: Pine needles, moss, patina copper

### Accent Colors

#### **Whiskey Amber** - `#CC8833`
- **Usage**: Highlights, featured items, special elements
- **Psychology**: Warmth, premium quality
- **Inspiration**: Aged bourbon, honey in sunlight

#### **Gunmetal Gray** - `#4A5459`
- **Usage**: Subtle elements, borders, disabled states
- **Psychology**: Industrial, modern, durable

### Background & Neutral Colors

- **Off-White Canvas**: `#F5F2ED` - Main backgrounds

## 2. Typography System

All fonts available via **Google Fonts** for easy implementation.

### Display/Heading Font

#### **Bebas Neue**
```html
<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Bebas+Neue&display=swap" rel="stylesheet">
```
- **Usage**: Main headlines, hero text, section headers
- **Character**: Bold, industrial, masculine, impactful
- **Weights**: Regular (400)
- **CSS**: `font-family: 'Bebas Neue', sans-serif;`

**Alternative**: **Oswald** (if you want multiple weights)
```html
<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Oswald:wght@400;500;700&display=swap" rel="stylesheet">
```

### Primary Body Font

#### **Work Sans**
```html
<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Work+Sans:wght@400;500;600;700&display=swap" rel="stylesheet">
```
- **Usage**: Body text, navigation, descriptions
- **Character**: Clean, modern, highly legible, professional
- **Weights**: Regular (400), Medium (500), SemiBold (600), Bold (700)
- **CSS**: `font-family: 'Work Sans', sans-serif;`

**Why Work Sans**:
- Excellent readability at all sizes
- Professional without being corporate
- Slightly geometric for a contemporary feel
- Great web performance

### Secondary/Accent Font

#### **Bitter** (Serif for emphasis)
```html
<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Bitter:wght@400;600;700&display=swap" rel="stylesheet">
```
- **Usage**: Pull quotes, product descriptions, storytelling elements
- **Character**: Sturdy, slab-serif, artisanal feel
- **Weights**: Regular (400), SemiBold (600), Bold (700)
- **CSS**: `font-family: 'Bitter', serif;`

**Alternative**: **Crimson Pro** for a more traditional, heritage feel

## 3. Logo Design Concept & Plan

### Logo Philosophy
The logo should embody:
- **Heritage**: Connection to ancestry and tradition
- **Craftsmanship**: Hand-made, artisan quality
- **Duality**: Partnership between "Paloma" (dove) and "Sage" (wisdom/plant)
- **Rugged Refinement**: Strong but sophisticated

### Logo Concept Options

#### **Option 1: Heraldic Badge**
- **Design**: Shield or badge shape with geometric dove silhouette
- **Elements**:
  - Simplified, angular dove (not soft/round)
  - Sage leaves integrated into border or corners
  - Strong, thick border with small decorative corners
  - Text: "PALOMA & SAGE" in Bebas Neue below/inside
  - Established date or location if applicable
- **Style**: Think brewery logos, vintage workshop stamps, leather embossing
- **Colors**: Single color (Charcoal Slate) or two-tone (Charcoal + Burnt Sienna)

#### **Option 2: Monogram Lock-Up**
- **Design**: Interlocking P&S letterforms
- **Elements**:
  - Bold, geometric letters
  - Dove silhouette incorporated into negative space
  - Sage sprig as decorative element
  - Can be used as standalone mark
- **Style**: Modern craftsman, like high-end tool brands
- **Colors**: Monochromatic for versatility

#### **Option 3: Illustrated Mark (Recommended)**
- **Design**: Stylized dove in flight carrying sage branch
- **Elements**:
  - Angular, woodcut-style illustration (not soft curves)
  - Strong line work, engraving aesthetic
  - Sage branch in beak or talons
  - Circular or rectangular frame
  - Text in Bebas Neue in arc or straight lockup
- **Style**: Vintage apothecary, old-world craftsmanship, tattoo art influence
- **Colors**: Black line art with selective color (Burnt Sienna on sage leaves)

### Logo Specifications

**Formats Needed**:
- Primary horizontal lockup
- Stacked vertical version
- Icon-only mark (for favicon, social media)
- Reversed/knockout version for dark backgrounds

**Sizing Guidelines**:
- Minimum size: 40px height (digital), 0.5" (print)
- Clear space: Equal to height of the letter "P" on all sides
- Preferred background: Off-white, wood textures, stone textures

**Do's**:
- Use on textured backgrounds (wood, canvas, stone)
- Pair with photography of raw materials
- Emboss or deboss effects

**Don'ts**:
- No gradients
- No soft drop shadows
- Don't stretch or skew
- Don't place on busy patterns

### Logo Implementation Tools

**DIY Options**:
1. **Canva Pro**: Use geometric shapes, upload custom fonts
2. **Inkscape**: Free vector software, full control
3. **Adobe Express**: Template-based with customization

**Professional Options**:
1. **Hire on Fiverr**: $50-200 for custom logo + variations
2. **99designs**: Design contest format, multiple options
3. **Local designer**: Full brand package

**Quick Start**: Use a temporary text-based logo with Bebas Neue while commissioning the illustrated version.

## 4. Visual Style Guidelines

### Photography Style
- **Subject**: Hands working, raw materials, process shots
- **Lighting**: Natural light, golden hour, dramatic shadows
- **Processing**: Slightly desaturated, warm tones, high contrast
- **Composition**: Close-ups, texture details, environmental shots
- **Mood**: Authentic, unpolished, honest

### Textures & Patterns
- **Use**: Wood grain, canvas weave, stone surfaces, leather texture
- **Application**: Subtle backgrounds, section dividers, hover states
- **Opacity**: 10-30% overlay on backgrounds

### Iconography
- **Style**: Line-based, consistent stroke width (2-3px)
- **Inspiration**: Hand tools, natural elements, geometric shapes
- **Avoid**: Rounded, cutesy, over-detailed

### UI Elements
- **Buttons**: Rectangular with subtle rounding (2-4px border-radius)
- **Borders**: 2-3px solid lines, double borders for emphasis
- **Shadows**: Hard-edged or minimal, avoid soft blurs
- **Spacing**: Generous whitespace, clear hierarchy

## 5. Voice & Tone

### Brand Voice
- **Authentic**: Honest, no-nonsense, transparent
- **Knowledgeable**: Expertise without arrogance
- **Grounded**: Connected to earth, materials, tradition
- **Confident**: Assured in quality and craft

### Writing Style
- Short, declarative sentences
- Active voice
- Specific over generic ("Hand-carved walnut" vs. "Made from wood")
- Stories over features

### Messaging Examples
- **Before**: "Welcome friends to an exploration of the Divine through art!"
- **After**: "Handcrafted goods honoring ancestral traditions. Every piece tells a story."

## 6. Competitive Differentiation

### This Aesthetic Avoids:
- ❌ Obvious camo patterns
- ❌ Excessive military motifs
- ❌ Aggressive, overly "tough guy" imagery
- ❌ Generic outdoor brand tropes
- ❌ Cheap Americana clichés

### This Aesthetic Embraces:
- ✅ Artisan craftsmanship
- ✅ Natural, earned patina
- ✅ Heritage with contemporary relevance
- ✅ Functional beauty
- ✅ Timeless over trendy

### Inspiration Brands (for reference):
- **Best Made Co.**: Rugged tools, heritage aesthetic
- **Filson**: Outdoor gear with refined sensibility
- **Shinola**: Detroit craftsmanship, American-made
- **Whiskey brands**: Woodford Reserve, Bulleit (typography & color)
- **Japanese workwear**: Kapital, Visvim (quality & detail)

## Conclusion

This brand design transforms Paloma & Sage into a **masculine, heritage-driven craft brand** that respects tradition while feeling contemporary. The color palette draws from earth, metal, and natural materials—not camo patterns, but the real textures that craftspeople work with daily.

The typography is bold and legible (Bebas Neue + Work Sans), the logo concept honors both partnership and craft, and the overall aesthetic says "quality over quantity, tradition over trends."
